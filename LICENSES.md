## Geographic and elevation data

- **OpenStreetMap**: Tuen Mun Road alignment and road context. Copyright OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the Open Database License 1.0. https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- **OSRM public routing service**: Used during authoring to select the connected Tuen Mun-to-Tsuen Wan alignment from OpenStreetMap data. The routed geometry is committed locally and is not requested while playing.
- **Copernicus DEM GLO-90 through Open-Meteo**: Preliminary elevation character. The profile is smoothed and vertically compressed for gameplay and is not survey-grade road elevation. https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/elevation-api
- **Hong Kong Lands Department Digital Terrain Model**: Five-metre terrain samples used to derive the compressed road and hillside profiles. https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/geoportal/?datasetId=landsd_rcd_1638158088368_93806
- **Hong Kong Lands Department Building dataset**: Route-corridor footprints and height attributes used to generate original low-poly building geometry. https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/geoportal/?datasetId=landsd_rcd_1637211194312_35158
- **Hong Kong Lands Department 3D Visualisation Map**: Non-textured and individualised datasets used as geographic reference for structures, terrain, coastline, and landmark relationships. No government model or texture is distributed.
- **Open3Dhk Streetscape 360**: Georeferenced visual reference only. No street-level image is included or redistributed. https://3d.map.gov.hk/?l=en-US

The Lands Department data is used under the applicable Hong Kong Government CSDI Terms of Use and Disclaimer. No Google Maps, Google Street View, government street imagery, or extracted third-party 3D imagery is distributed with the game.

## Game content

All game geometry, materials, interface styling, music tones, and sound effects are generated in code for this project. No third-party art, models, textures, music recordings, or sound recordings are distributed. The route is a compressed recreation and is not intended for navigation.
